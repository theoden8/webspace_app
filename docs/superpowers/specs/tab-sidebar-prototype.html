Tab sidebar

A clickable prototype of the design. Pick how sites are shown under Site navigation: the Tab button (today's: the tab count in the app bar opens the Tabs sheet), the Site strip, where each site opens out to show its tabs, or the Sidebar, the same tabs as a tree down the left. In full screen picks whether it hides, stays, or comes back through a small accessory. Double-tap the site's name, or use the ⋮ menu, for full screen; the handle at the top centre or Esc leaves it, as in the app. Animations use the app's timings; set them to Slow to study them. Example sites, not your data.
