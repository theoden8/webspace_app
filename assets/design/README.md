# Design artifacts

Source artwork produced through the Claude Design pipeline (see
[tool/design_gallery/CLAUDE.md](../../tool/design_gallery/CLAUDE.md)): the files
a designer authors and the app or its listings then consume.

Covered by [../LICENSE](../LICENSE), the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 grant as the rest
of `assets/`, because the designer is the author it already names. That is the
only reason it holds. An artifact by a different author does not belong under
someone else's copyright line: give it its own directory and its own licence
file, and record where it came from.

Rendered cards and screenshots under `build/` are derivatives of whatever they
depict and are not committed.

None of this is a settled licensing policy. It is the smallest arrangement that
is not wrong today, and it will need revisiting the first time authorship is not
implicit: joint authorship, machine-generated artwork with unclear terms, or a
contribution that arrives without terms attached.
