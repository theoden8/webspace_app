# Design artifacts

Source artwork produced through the Claude Design pipeline (see
[tool/design_gallery/CLAUDE.md](../../tool/design_gallery/CLAUDE.md)): the
files a designer authors and the app or its listings then consume.

Everything here carries [LICENSE](LICENSE) in this directory, not the app's
MIT licence and not the artwork licence one level up. Two reasons the split is
worth the extra file: the parent `assets/LICENSE` names a specific author whose
copyright these are not, and design work has different reuse terms from code.

**Scope.** Artifacts that come out of the design pipeline land here and take
this licence by default. Artwork from anywhere else is case by case: give it
its own directory and its own licence file, and record where it came from.
Rendered cards and screenshots under `build/` are derivatives of whatever they
depict and are not committed at all.
